Who We Are

Bodhi Tree Academy is dedicated to teaching martial arts, discipline, respect and self-confidence in a supportive learning environment.

Our aim is to help students develop physical fitness, concentration, self-control and the confidence to face everyday challenges.

We encourage students to learn Karate, improve their fitness and develop strong values through regular practice.
